Fresh Recs for YouTube — Privacy Policy

Fresh Recs does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

Everything the extension remembers stays in your browser's local extension storage, on your device:

  • the IDs of YouTube videos you've been recommended, how many times, and when
  • the IDs of videos you've opened or hidden with the ✕ button
  • your settings

None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or tracking.

You can erase the stored history at any time from the extension's popup ("Reset history" and "Unhide videos I hid"), or by removing the extension.
